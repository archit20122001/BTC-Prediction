PLATFORM 
 
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY 
BTC Binary Prediction Trading Platform is a decentralized peer-to-peer prediction marketplace 
built as a Telegram Mini App. 
The platform enables users to predict the direction of Bitcoin (BTCUSD Spot) candles within 
predefined time periods. 
Users participate by selecting one of two outcomes: 
ÔÇó 
Green Candle (Bullish) 
ÔÇó 
Red Candle (Bearish) 
Users stake USDT into their selected prediction side. 
After candle completion, the losing side's funds are distributed to the winning side according to 
predefined platform rules. 
Unlike traditional exchanges where users trade against the platform, this platform follows a user-
versus-user prediction model. 
The platform only facilitates: 
ÔÇó 
Pool creation 
ÔÇó 
Fund management 
ÔÇó 
Settlement calculations 
ÔÇó 
Referral distribution 
ÔÇó 
Reward pool distribution 
ÔÇó 
Audit tracking 
The platform does not take market exposure risk.

Green Candle Result
